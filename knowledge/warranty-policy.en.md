# Warranty Policy

## 1. Warranty period

Warranty starts on the delivery date of your order. The standard warranty period is **12 months**. Some categories have a different period:

| Category | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Electronics (default) | 12 months |
| Accessories | 6 months |
| Appliances | 24 months |

## 2. What the warranty covers

- Manufacturing defects and faults that appear under normal use during the warranty period.
- Repair or replacement of the defective part or unit, at our choice.

## 3. What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage such as drops, cracks and bent parts.
- Liquid damage, and damage from use outside the product's specifications.
- Repairs or modifications made by anyone other than an authorised service centre.
- Normal wear, including batteries that lose capacity and cosmetic scratches.
- Products whose serial number label is removed or altered.

## 4. How to claim warranty

1. Tell the support assistant your order ID, the product and a description of the problem.
2. The assistant checks that the product is still within its warranty period and prepares a warranty request for you to confirm.
3. A staff member reviews the request and gives you instructions for sending or bringing the product to a service centre.

## 5. Repair times

Repairs take **7 to 15 business days** from the day the service centre receives the product. If the product cannot be repaired, we replace it with an equivalent product or, if none is available, refund the purchase price.

## 6. Warranty after repair or replacement

A repaired product keeps the remaining part of its original warranty. A replacement product receives a new 12-month warranty.
