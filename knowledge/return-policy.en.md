# Return and Exchange Policy

## 1. Return window

You can return most items within **7 days of delivery**. The 7 days are counted from the date your order was marked as delivered, not from the date you placed it. Requests made after the 7-day window are not eligible for return or refund.

## 2. Conditions for returns

An item can be returned when all of the following are true:

- The order belongs to your account and its status is "Delivered".
- The item is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging with all accessories, tags and the invoice.
- The item is not on the non-returnable list in section 3.
- There is no other return or refund request already pending or approved for the same item.

## 3. Items that cannot be returned

- Gift cards and digital vouchers (category: gift_card).
- Items made to order or personalised for you.
- Opened personal-care and hygiene products, and underwear.

## 4. How to request a return

1. Tell the support assistant which order and which item you want to return, and why.
2. The assistant checks eligibility and prepares a return request for you to confirm.
3. A staff member reviews the request, usually within 1 business day, and approves or rejects it.
4. After approval, ship the item back using the instructions you receive.

Return shipping is free if the item arrived damaged or defective. In every other case the customer pays the return shipping fee.

## 5. Refunds

- Refunds are issued within **5 to 7 business days** after we receive the returned item and the request is approved.
- The money goes back to the original payment method. For cash on delivery (COD) orders, the refund is sent by bank transfer to the account you provide.
- Refund requests above **500,000 VND** are reviewed with priority by a senior staff member before they are approved.
- Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was defective or sent in error.

## 6. Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product within the same 7-day window, subject to stock availability. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

## 7. Items that arrive damaged or defective

Report damaged or defective items within the 7-day return window with a photo of the problem. We cover the return shipping and offer a replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping fee.
