# Shipping Policy

## 1. Delivery times

- **Inner-city Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City:** 2 to 3 business days.
- **Other provinces:** 4 to 7 business days.
- **Same-day delivery:** available in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City for orders confirmed before 12:00 noon, for an extra fee.

Delivery times are counted from the moment the order is handed to the carrier, not from the time of ordering. Orders are usually handed over within 1 business day after payment is confirmed.

## 2. Shipping fees

- Orders of **300,000 VND or more** ship free of charge (standard delivery).
- Orders below 300,000 VND: 30,000 VND for inner-city Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, 40,000 VND for other provinces.
- Same-day delivery costs an extra 25,000 VND on top of the standard fee.

## 3. Tracking your order

Once the order is handed to the carrier you receive a tracking code by email and SMS. You can also ask the support assistant "Where is my order?" with your order ID to see the carrier, tracking code, current status and the estimated delivery date.

Order statuses are: processing, shipping, delivered and cancelled.

## 4. Changing the address or cancelling

You can change the delivery address or cancel an order for free while its status is **processing**. Once the order is in **shipping**, the address can no longer be changed and the order can only be refused at the door or returned under the return policy.

## 5. Failed deliveries

The carrier tries to deliver up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the parcel is sent back to our warehouse. We then contact you to re-ship (you pay the shipping fee again) or to cancel the order with a refund.

## 6. Delays

If your order has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support with your order ID and we will investigate with the carrier.
