# Payment Policy

## 1. Accepted payment methods

- **Cash on delivery (COD):** pay the carrier when you receive the order.
- **Bank transfer:** transfer to our account using your order ID as the transfer note.
- **Credit and debit cards:** Visa and Mastercard.
- **E-wallets:** MoMo, ZaloPay and VNPay.

## 2. Cash on delivery limits

COD is available for orders up to **5,000,000 VND**. Larger orders must be paid by bank transfer, card or e-wallet.

## 3. Payment confirmation

- Card and e-wallet payments are confirmed instantly.
- Bank transfers are confirmed within 30 minutes during business hours (08:00 to 21:00). Transfers made outside business hours are confirmed the next morning.
- The order moves to "processing" only after payment is confirmed (COD orders move to processing right after you place them).

## 4. Installments

Orders of **3,000,000 VND or more** paid with a supported credit card can be split into 3 or 6 monthly installments at 0% interest. Debit cards and e-wallets are not eligible for installments.

## 5. VAT invoices

Request a VAT invoice within 24 hours of placing the order by giving your company name, tax code and address. Invoices cannot be issued after the order has been delivered.

## 6. Payment safety

We never ask for your card password, OTP code or full card number through chat, phone or email. If someone asks for them in our name, do not share them and contact support.

## 7. Failed or duplicate payments

If money was deducted but the order shows as unpaid, wait 30 minutes and then contact support with your order ID and a payment screenshot. Duplicate payments are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
